## Recorded result

After a fresh package and user-provided Linux ARM64 Cubism Core, the migrated game launched an X11 window and rendered its initial important-notice screen.

## Limitations and current release scope

Earlier extraction/crash reports led to packaging and SDK fixes. **Gameplay, Live2D animation and save/load remain unverified**. Requires separately obtained vendor Cubism SDK under its terms. This record includes results known by the **v0.1.0.3** release, not a blanket promise of v0.1.0.3 end-to-end gameplay verification.
